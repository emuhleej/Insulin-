Starting and potassium

Do not initiate until serum potassium is at least 3.3 mEq/L. Hold if below 3.3; resume when at least 3.3. The order states a starting rate of 4 units/hr, then adjustment per the algorithm. The restart rate after a potassium hold is not specified.

During a running infusion

  • Glucose 120–139 mg/dL or a decrease greater than 75 mg/dL: decrease by the table amount.
  • Glucose decrease of 50–75 mg/dL or glucose 140–180 mg/dL: continue the current rate.
  • Glucose greater than 180 mg/dL that has not decreased by at least 50 mg/dL: increase by the table amount.
  • Glucose 100–119 mg/dL: decrease by 50%.

Low glucose and resuming

Below 100 mg/dL: stop insulin. Check glucose hourly until greater than 120 mg/dL, then restart at 50% of the previous rate. Below 70 mg/dL: stop insulin, use the hypoglycemia protocol, and notify the provider. That separate treatment protocol is not supplied here.

Documentation

Record the hourly insulin rate with hourly point-of-care glucose checks, even if unchanged. Record 0 units/hr when the infusion is turned off.

When this dashboard asks you to clarify

Conflicting adjustment clauses; a rate between 9 and 10 when a table-based adjustment is needed; an unspecified restart reason/rate; starting at glucose 100–139; a nonpositive calculated rate; or checks that are not consecutive hourly readings. No pump rounding is assumed. Verify the calculated precision against your order and pump.
